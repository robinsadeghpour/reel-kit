Everything here is a default: what I ask for in the moment wins.

Lines marked (default) came with the kit. They are what one creator settled on after
sending real cuts back, so they are a reasonable place to start, not rules. Change or
delete any of them.

## Words

- Hesitation sounds ("um", "uh") and phrases said twice in a row are removed. (default)
- My style words stay in: _(list the words that are just how you talk, e.g. "honestly", "like", "basically")_
- Never rewrite what I say. If a sentence is factually off, tell me; do not cut around it to change its meaning. (default)
- Cut for watch time: I would rather lose sentences and have them reordered into a story that hooks than keep everything in the order I said it. Tell me what went. (default)

## Sound

- No loud breaths, no hesitation pauses. (default)
- Loudness around -15 LUFS, peaks at or below -2 dBFS. (default)
- My voice and a clip's own sound never play together. They take turns. (default)
- Sound effects: quiet and short, tied to a graphic appearing. Tell me which play where. (default)
- Music: none added. (default)

## Picture

- Captions: off, I add them in the app. _(or: on; say where on screen and how they should look)_ (default)
- No colour grade. Leave the footage as the camera recorded it. (default)
- Zoom cuts for emphasis: punch in on the phrase that carries the point. (default)
- Something should change on screen by about three seconds in, and regularly after. (default)
- Supporting visuals so it is not only my face: a number sticker for a figure I say, a card with the real page of a product I name, a photo of a thing I mention. (default)
- My head stays in view where possible: prefer a card above me to replacing me, except when a clip is the moment. (default)
- When the video is about something you can watch, let it play for several seconds with its own sound. (default)
- Colours and typeface: the kit's look. _(or: set "theme" and "font" in reel.json to your brand's)_
